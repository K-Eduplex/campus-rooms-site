Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Campus Rooms is an internal tool used by JIU (jiu.ac, k-eduplex.net) and CGA (cga.sch.id) staff to view and reserve shared campus rooms. This page explains what information the tool uses and how it is handled.

Who operates this tool

The tool is operated by the JIU campus administration. Contact: its@k-eduplex.net.

Information we use

  • Google account identity. When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and display name. We use them only to identify you, to check that your account belongs to one of the participating schools, and to record who made or approved a booking.
  • Booking details you enter. Room, date and time, title (purpose), optional purpose grade, expected headcount, notes, and the reason for a preemption request.
  • Calendar data. The tool reads the shared room calendars in the schools' Google Workspace to show availability, and writes bookings to them as calendar events that invite you and the room.

We do not request access to your personal calendar, contacts, files, or any other Google data. We do not use cookies for tracking; the only browser storage used is a sign-in session (up to 12 hours), a cached staff directory and your recent invitees for attendee suggestions, and a copy of the booking board and your profile so the page opens quickly. Signing out removes all of it from the browser.

How the information is used

  • To display room availability and to create, approve, reject, or cancel bookings.
  • To send email notifications about your requests to you and to the room's approvers.
  • To post a one-line notice of confirmed or cancelled bookings (room, time, title, and requester name) to the schools' internal Google Chat space.
  • To keep an audit log of booking actions for the school administration.

Where it is stored

All data stays inside the schools' Google Workspace: Google Calendar (bookings), a Google Sheet owned by the administration (pending requests and the audit log), and Google Apps Script (the backend). Nothing is sent to third-party services. The public website only contains the user interface; it stores no personal data.

Who can see it

Booking titles and requester names are visible to signed-in staff of the participating schools on the availability board, as they are in the shared room calendars today. Reasons for preemption requests and rejection notes are visible only to the requester, the room's approvers, and administrators.

Retention

Calendar events remain in the room calendars according to the schools' calendar policies. Request records and audit logs are kept for administrative purposes and may be deleted by the administration. Sign-in sessions expire after 12 hours.

Your choices

To offer name suggestions when you invite attendees, the tool also keeps a directory list of names and email addresses. It is filled from your organisation's staff directory, from people who sign in, and from lists an administrator adds by hand — so a name can appear there before that person has used the tool. Nothing else about those people is stored.

You may cancel your own bookings and requests at any time from "My bookings". To ask for correction or deletion of your data, contact its@k-eduplex.net.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Continued use of the tool after a change means you accept the updated policy.
